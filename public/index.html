Find and collect

Enter an exact nine-digit UIC or eight-digit source API. Longer API formats are unsupported. Choose a source candidate explicitly and confirm 1–36 inclusive reporting months. A 25-candidate cap, duplicate parent or 1,000-report cap blocks completeness.

Collection creates a new immutable version. Partial failures retain available evidence. The server returns terminal run records; live sub-stage progress is unavailable.

Understand the records

Inventory rows, wells, monthly reports and distinct reporting months are different counts. Accepted records passed the recorded checks; they do not establish engineering readiness, compliance or safe conditions.

Missing is not zero. No reports returned is distinct from history not acquired. Pressure is reported injection psi, not BHP. Calendar-day rate divides monthly liquid barrels by calendar days; it is not a measured daily operating rate. Permit limits are regulatory constraints.

Take evidence into Analytics

Open a verified dataset, choose Downloads, then Use in Injection Analytics. A new local window validates the whole package and shows a preview before you confirm session-only adoption. You can also download the complete TAR or extract its entire directory for the Analytics Node CLI verifier.

Generic CSV/XLSX import is a separate workflow with unverified profile mapping. Transfer requires the configured local apps. There is no automatic adoption or default-pin change; recorded collection evidence does not become an Analytics acknowledgement. Public injection records are not produced-water observations or calibrated injectivity/BHP data.

Coverage and provenance

Each version states its exact UIC, reporting bounds, original identifiers, retrieval times and source hashes. Retrieval time does not establish the reporting period or current operating status.

There is no statewide coverage, GIS, scheduler, annulus acquisition or original H-10 document revision ledger. Quality issues remain inspectable; no manual override is offered. This explorer is not an official RRC product.

Human-readable timestamps use your browser timezone; downloaded evidence retains its original UTC timestamps.
